# Product Brand and Design

Derive design from product domain, target user, task frequency, trust needs, device context, and market research. In the design report, describe visual direction, information hierarchy, core screen flows, responsive behavior, accessibility, semantic tokens, and meaningful loading/empty/error/success states. Propose at least two original brand/logo/wordmark directions with rationale and recommend one. Explain color, type, icon, spacing, and logo behavior on light/dark and small screens. Use references as inspiration with direct URLs; never copy logos or create generic marks without product rationale. Avoid defaulting to dark mode, purple gradients, repeated cards, bento grids, glass, 3D, or motion unless they solve a stated user need. Motion honors `prefers-reduced-motion` and keyboard/focus behavior.
