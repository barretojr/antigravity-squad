# Product Research

For product discovery and business features, inspect 3–5 relevant products and authoritative domain references. Prefer primary sources: official product/help/pricing pages, standards bodies, and official technical docs. Record direct URLs and access dates; state the specific behavior/pattern each source supports. Compare user journeys, onboarding/authentication, search/filtering, monetization, empty/error states, and trust signals only when relevant. Mark facts, inference, and recommendation separately. Do not claim research without sources; if web access is unavailable, mark the relevant questions pending. Never copy competitor branding or protected content.
